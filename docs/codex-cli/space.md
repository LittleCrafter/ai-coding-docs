# ChatGPT Space

> For the complete documentation index, see [llms.txt](https://learn.chatgpt.com/llms.txt). Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to the page URL.

ChatGPT Space brings your pages, files, and shared work into one place. Write and
edit alongside ChatGPT, then invite other people to work with you.

> Illustration: Welcome to Space, with mock documents and animated collaborator cursors. Move your pointer over the illustration to see a cursor labeled You.

## Workspaces and spaces

Your **ChatGPT workspace** is the personal or organization environment you're
signed in to. It determines your membership, settings, and available apps.
Space holds the work you can access in that environment.

Within Space, a **space** groups pages around a topic or team, such as a design
studio or a research project. Share the space to give collaborators access to
its pages. You can also create a page on its own.

> Illustration: Space with a sidebar for Pages, Sites, Images, and Google Drive, and a list of saved work.

## Pages and other content

A **[page](./space/pages.md)** is a document you and ChatGPT can edit together.
It can hold notes, a plan, or a team handbook, with child pages for related work.
You can write directly, ask ChatGPT for revisions, and leave comments for
collaborators.

Space also brings together:

- **Spreadsheets and presentations:** Create and edit tables, calculations, and
  slides with ChatGPT.
- **[Sites](./sites.md):** Websites and tools you build with ChatGPT.
- **Images and files:** Uploaded files and saved work you can open and reuse.

## Connect your sources

Connected apps, such as Google Drive, let ChatGPT use files you already have
access to. Add source links or files to a page, then ask ChatGPT to turn that
context into a summary, comparison, or update. Available sources depend on your
[connected apps](./plugins.md#overview) and workspace settings.

Linked files keep their original permissions. Content you copy or summarize
onto a page is visible to people who can access that page. Sharing a page
doesn't share your private chats or saved memory.

To create your first page, see [Getting started](./space/getting-started.md).
For workspace controls, see [Manage ChatGPT Space](./enterprise/chatgpt-space.md).
